How It Started

Born From a Kitchen Experiment

In 2018, our founder Maya Torres was working 60-hour weeks in corporate finance. Exhausted, unhealthy, and fed up with fast food, she started blending her own juices at home — experimenting with turmeric, ginger, and whatever fresh produce she could find.

Friends started asking for her blends. Then their friends. Within months, Maya was blending 50+ drinks a week from her tiny Brooklyn apartment kitchen. The demand was real — and so was the passion.

She quit her job, took a leap of faith, and opened the first Smoothix counter in a 200 sq ft space on Orchard Lane. Six years later, we have three locations, 15 team members, and over 250,000 blends served.
